## Session Overview

이번 세션에서는 돈이 없는 상태를 나타내는 broke부터 가격을 평가하는 pricey, overpriced, good value, 씀씀이를 묘사하는 thrifty·extravagant, 그리고 on the house·split the bill·get your money's worth 같은 실용 표현까지 총 9개의 청크를 배웠습니다. 카페, 식당, 여행, 쇼핑 등 다양한 일상 상황에서 바로 쓸 수 있는 표현들로 구성되어 있습니다. 이 표현들을 익히면 영어로 가격과 소비에 대해 훨씬 자연스럽게 이야기할 수 있습니다.

## 📦 Phrasal Verbs & Chunks

### Broke — 돈이 쪼들리는

- *I can't go out tonight — I'm completely broke.*
  - 오늘 밤 못 나가 — 나 완전히 돈이 쪼들려.

> **A:** Want to grab dinner at that new steakhouse?
>
> **B:** I'm broke until payday, so let's go somewhere cheaper.

### Pricey — 값이 나가는

- *That new café downtown is a bit pricey for a coffee.*
  - 시내 그 새 카페는 커피 한 잔치고는 좀 값이 나가.

> **A:** How was the hotel you stayed at?
>
> **B:** Amazing views, but way too pricey — once was enough.

### Overpriced — 바가지 쓴

- *The souvenirs near the Eiffel Tower are completely overpriced.*
  - 에펠탑 근처 기념품들은 완전히 바가지야.

> **A:** Did you buy anything at the tourist market?
>
> **B:** No, everything was overpriced — totally not worth it.

### Good value — 가성비 좋은

- *This hostel is good value — clean beds and free breakfast included.*
  - 이 호스텔은 가성비가 좋아 — 깨끗한 침대에 조식도 무료야.

> **A:** Is that meal-kit subscription worth trying?
>
> **B:** Definitely — it's good value for the quality you get.

### Thrifty — 알뜰한

- *She's so thrifty — she always compares prices before buying anything.*
  - 그녀는 정말 알뜰해 — 항상 구매 전에 가격을 비교해.

> **A:** How does he afford to travel so often?
>
> **B:** He's thrifty — early bookings and no fancy hotels.

### Extravagant — 사치스러운

- *Renting a private villa for one night felt a little extravagant.*
  - 하룻밤에 프라이빗 빌라를 빌리는 건 좀 사치스럽게 느껴졌어.

> **A:** They ordered the full tasting menu for two!
>
> **B:** So extravagant — that must have cost a fortune.

### On the house — 공짜인

- *The manager smiled and said dessert was on the house.*
  - 매니저가 웃으며 디저트는 서비스라고 했어.

> **A:** Do I owe you for that glass of wine?
>
> **B:** Not at all — it's on the house, welcome to the neighborhood!

### Split the bill — 더치페이하다

- *Let's just split the bill — it's easier than calculating shares.*
  - 그냥 더치페이하자 — 각자 계산하는 것보다 더 편해.

> **A:** Who's covering dinner tonight?
>
> **B:** Let's split the bill so no one feels uncomfortable.

### Get your money's worth — 본전 뽑다

- *With the all-you-can-eat buffet, I really got my money's worth.*
  - 무한리필 뷔페에서 완전히 본전을 뽑았어.

> **A:** Was the concert ticket worth the price?
>
> **B:** Totally — they played three hours, I got my money's worth.
