## Session Overview

이번 세션에서는 상대방에게 경고하거나 충고를 전달하고, 감정을 표현하거나 조절하는 데 쓰이는 구어체 청크 12개를 학습했습니다. 'Don't play with fire', 'Mark my words'처럼 강한 경고 표현부터 'Bear with me', 'I'll let it slide'처럼 상황을 부드럽게 무마하는 표현까지 폭넓게 다루었습니다. 모두 원어민이 일상 대화에서 즐겨 쓰는 관용 표현으로, 실전 대화에서 바로 활용할 수 있습니다.

## 📦 Phrasal Verbs & Chunks

### I'll let it slide — 넘어가 줄게

- *You're late again, but I'll let it slide this time.*
  - 또 늦었네, 하지만 이번엔 넘어가 줄게.

> **A:** I forgot to submit the report on time. I'm so sorry.
>
> **B:** I'll let it slide, but don't let it happen again.

### Bear with me — 조금만 참아줘

- *Bear with me while I find the right file.*
  - 파일 찾는 동안 조금만 참아줘.

> **A:** This presentation is taking forever to load.
>
> **B:** Bear with me — it should be up in a second.

### Level with me — 솔직하게 말해줘

- *Come on, level with me — what really happened?*
  - 솔직하게 말해줘 — 실제로 무슨 일이 있었어?

> **A:** I need to know if the project is really on track.
>
> **B:** Okay, I'll level with you — we're behind schedule.

### Back to square one — 원점으로 돌아가자

- *The client rejected the design, so we're back to square one.*
  - 클라이언트가 디자인을 거절해서 우린 원점으로 돌아가게 됐어.

> **A:** Did they approve the new proposal?
>
> **B:** No — it's back to square one. They want something totally different.

### Take a step back — 한발 물러서자

- *Let's take a step back and think about this more carefully.*
  - 한발 물러서서 이걸 좀 더 신중하게 생각해 보자.

> **A:** I just want to quit everything right now.
>
> **B:** Take a step back — you'll feel differently after some rest.

### Don't jump to conclusions — 속단하지 마

- *Don't jump to conclusions before you hear the full story.*
  - 전체 이야기를 듣기 전에 속단하지 마.

> **A:** He didn't text back — I think he's upset with me.
>
> **B:** Don't jump to conclusions; he's probably just busy.

### Get in line — 줄 서세요

- *You'll need to get in line if you want tickets for the show.*
  - 공연 티켓을 원하면 줄을 서야 해요.

> **A:** Can I go before you? I'm really in a hurry.
>
> **B:** Sorry, you'll have to get in line like everyone else.

### That does it — 더는 못 참아

- *That does it — I'm calling the manager right now.*
  - 더는 못 참아 — 지금 바로 매니저를 부를 거야.

> **A:** They canceled our reservation again without any notice.
>
> **B:** That does it — we're never booking with them again.

### Mark my words — 내 말 명심해

- *Mark my words, this team will win the championship.*
  - 내 말 명심해, 이 팀이 우승할 거야.

> **A:** Do you really think he'll regret quitting?
>
> **B:** Mark my words — he'll be back within six months.

### Bite your tongue — 말 함부로 하지 마

- *Just bite your tongue and don't say anything you'll regret.*
  - 말 함부로 하지 말고 후회할 말은 하지 마.

> **A:** I really want to tell my boss exactly what I think.
>
> **B:** Bite your tongue — you still need that job.

### Don't play with fire — 위험한 짓 마

- *Don't play with fire by investing all your savings in one stock.*
  - 모든 저축을 한 종목에 투자하는 위험한 짓은 하지 마.

> **A:** I'm thinking of confronting him alone tonight.
>
> **B:** Don't play with fire — let the police handle it.

### Get a grip — 정신 차려

- *You need to get a grip and stop panicking about the exam.*
  - 정신 차리고 시험 때문에 패닉하는 것 좀 그만해.

> **A:** I can't stop worrying — everything is falling apart!
>
> **B:** Get a grip; things aren't as bad as they seem.

## ✅ Check Yourself

**Q1.** I know you're frustrated, but just ____ — we'll figure this out together.

- Bear with me
- That does it
- Get in line

<details><summary>Show answer</summary><p><strong>Bear with me</strong> — 'Bear with me'는 잠깐만 참아달라는 부탁으로 문맥에 딱 맞음. 'That does it'은 분노가 한계에 달한 표현, 'Get in line'은 줄 서라는 지시라 부적절.</p></details>

**Q2.** The whole plan fell apart, so it's ____.

- Back to square one
- Take a step back
- Mark my words

<details><summary>Show answer</summary><p><strong>Back to square one</strong> — 계획이 완전히 무너져 처음부터 다시 시작해야 하는 상황이므로 'Back to square one'이 정답. 'Take a step back'은 한발 물러서라는 뜻, 'Mark my words'는 내 말 명심하라는 뜻이라 문맥 불일치.</p></details>

**Q3.** She's been lying to me for months — ____! I'm done with this relationship.

- I'll let it slide
- That does it
- Bite your tongue

<details><summary>Show answer</summary><p><strong>That does it</strong> — 오래 쌓인 분노가 터지는 순간에 '더는 못 참아'의 'That does it'이 정답. 'I'll let it slide'는 넘어가 준다는 뜻, 'Bite your tongue'은 말을 삼키라는 뜻이라 맞지 않음.</p></details>

**Q4.** You don't know the full story yet, so ____.

- Don't jump to conclusions

- Level with me

<details><summary>Show answer</summary><p><strong>Don't jump to conclusions</strong> — 아직 상황을 다 모르는데 성급히 결론 내리려는 사람을 말리는 표현이므로 'Don't jump to conclusions'이 정답. 'Get a grip'은 정신 차리라는 뜻, 'Level with me'는 솔직히 말해달라는 뜻이라 맞지 않음.</p></details>

- Get a grip
