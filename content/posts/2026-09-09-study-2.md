## Session Overview

## 📦 Phrasal Verbs & Chunks

### Sluggish — 몸이 찌뿌둥한

- *I feel so sluggish after sleeping in all morning.*
  - 늦잠을 자고 났더니 몸이 너무 찌뿌둥해.

> **A:** You look tired. Are you feeling okay?
>
> **B:** Yeah, I just feel really sluggish today.

### Nauseous — 속이 미식거리는

- *She felt nauseous after riding the roller coaster.*
  - 롤러코스터를 타고 나서 속이 미식거렸다.

> **A:** Why did you leave the meeting so early?
>
> **B:** I started feeling nauseous and needed fresh air.

### Dizzy — 어지러운

- *He got dizzy after standing up too quickly.*
  - 너무 빨리 일어났다가 어지러워졌다.

> **A:** Are you alright? You look really pale.
>
> **B:** I'm a bit dizzy — I haven't eaten all day.

### Stuffy — 코가 막힌

- *My nose has been stuffy since yesterday morning.*
  - 어제 아침부터 코가 계속 막혀 있어.

> **A:** You sound different — do you have a cold?
>
> **B:** Yeah, I'm all stuffy and can barely breathe.

### Hoarse — 목이 쉰

- *Her voice was hoarse after cheering at the game all night.*
  - 밤새 경기에서 응원하고 나서 목이 쉬었다.

> **A:** Why are you whispering? Are you okay?
>
> **B:** My voice is hoarse — I've been talking all day.

### Bruised — 멍이 든

- *His arm was badly bruised after the fall.*
  - 넘어진 후 팔에 멍이 심하게 들었다.

> **A:** What happened to your leg?
>
> **B:** I bumped into a table — it's bruised but fine.

### Sober up — 술이 깨다

- *Drink some water and eat — it'll help you sober up.*
  - 물 마시고 뭐라도 먹어. 술 깨는 데 도움이 될 거야.

> **A:** I need to drive home in about an hour.
>
> **B:** Then stop drinking now and sober up first.
