## Session Overview

이번 세션에서는 have, take, make 세 동사를 활용한 일상 영어 청크 12개를 학습했습니다. '실컷 울다(have a good cry)', '잠깐 휴식을 취하다(take a quick break)', '큰 차이를 만들다(make a big difference)' 등 감정 표현부터 행동, 의사결정에 이르기까지 폭넓은 상황에서 쓸 수 있는 표현들을 익혔습니다. 각 청크를 실제 대화 맥락 속에서 반복 연습하면 자연스럽고 원어민다운 영어 표현력을 기를 수 있습니다.

## 📦 Phrasal Verbs & Chunks

### have a good cry — 실컷 울다

- *After the tough news, she went home and had a good cry.*
  - 힘든 소식을 들은 후 그녀는 집에 가서 실컷 울었다.

> **A:** You've been holding it together all day — are you okay?
>
> **B:** Honestly, I just need to go home and have a good cry.

### have a good sleep — 숙면을 취하다

- *I finally had a good sleep after a long, stressful week.*
  - 길고 힘든 한 주를 보낸 후 드디어 숙면을 취했다.

> **A:** You look so much more refreshed than yesterday!
>
> **B:** I had a good sleep for the first time in weeks.

### have a good talk — 진지하게 대화를 나누다

- *We really need to have a good talk about our future plans.*
  - 우리 미래 계획에 대해 진지하게 대화를 나눠야 해.

> **A:** Things between us have felt off lately.
>
> **B:** I agree — let's have a good talk tonight after dinner.

### take a look — 한번 살펴보다

- *Could you take a look at my report before I submit it?*
  - 제출하기 전에 제 보고서를 한번 살펴봐 줄 수 있어?

> **A:** Something seems wrong with this code but I can't find it.
>
> **B:** I'll take a look — send it over to me.

### take a quick break — 잠깐 휴식을 취하다

- *Let's take a quick break before we start the next section.*
  - 다음 부분을 시작하기 전에 잠깐 휴식을 취하자.

> **A:** We've been studying for two hours straight.
>
> **B:** Good point — let's take a quick break and grab some water.

### take a moment — 잠깐 시간을 갖다 / 진정할 시간을 갖다

- *Please take a moment to read through the contract carefully.*
  - 잠깐 시간을 갖고 계약서를 꼼꼼히 읽어보세요.

> **A:** Can you give me your answer right now?
>
> **B:** Can I take a moment? It's a pretty big decision.

### take another moment — 시간이 조금 더 필요하다

- *If you need to take another moment, please don't rush.*
  - 시간이 조금 더 필요하다면 서두르지 않아도 됩니다.

> **A:** Are you ready to share your answer with the group?
>
> **B:** Actually, could I take another moment? I'm still thinking.

### make a good decision — 좋은 결정을 내리다

- *She made a good decision to study abroad.*
  - 그녀는 해외 유학을 가기로 좋은 결정을 내렸다.

> **A:** I finally accepted the safer job offer.
>
> **B:** You made a good decision — that company is stable.

### make a big mistake — 큰 실수를 하다

- *He made a big mistake by ignoring the warning.*
  - 그는 경고를 무시해서 큰 실수를 했다.

> **A:** I forgot to back up all my files.
>
> **B:** Oh no, you made a big mistake.

### make a quick plan — 간단하게 빨리 계획을 짜다

- *Let's make a quick plan before the meeting starts.*
  - 회의가 시작하기 전에 빨리 계획을 짜자.

> **A:** We only have ten minutes before they arrive.
>
> **B:** Okay, let's make a quick plan right now.

### make a smart choice — 현명한 선택을 하다

- *You made a smart choice by saving money early.*
  - 일찍부터 저축하기로 해서 현명한 선택을 했어.

> **A:** I decided to cut back on eating out.
>
> **B:** You made a smart choice for your budget.

### make a big difference — 큰 차이를 만들다

- *Regular exercise makes a big difference in your health.*
  - 규칙적인 운동은 건강에 큰 차이를 만든다.

> **A:** Does getting enough sleep really matter that much?
>
> **B:** Absolutely — it makes a big difference every day.

## ✅ Check Yourself

**Q1.** After the breakup, she went home and just needed to ____.

- have a good cry
- have a good sleep
- make a quick plan
- take a look

<details><summary>Show answer</summary><p><strong>have a good cry</strong> — 이별 후 감정을 쏟아내야 하는 상황이므로 have a good cry(실컷 울다)가 맞고, have a good sleep은 '자다', take a look은 '살펴보다'라 문맥에 맞지 않음.</p></details>

**Q2.** The doctor said the best thing I can do right now is ____.

- have a good sleep
- take a quick break
- have a good talk
- make a big mistake

<details><summary>Show answer</summary><p><strong>have a good sleep</strong> — 몸 회복을 위한 의사의 조언이므로 have a good sleep(숙면을 취하다)이 맞고, take a quick break는 가벼운 휴식이라 의학적 권고로는 뉘앙스가 부족함.</p></details>

**Q3.** Can you ____ at my resume before I send it out?

- take a look
- take a moment
- make a smart choice
- have a good cry

<details><summary>Show answer</summary><p><strong>take a look</strong> — 이력서 검토를 부탁하는 상황이므로 take a look(한번 살펴보다)이 맞고, take a moment는 'take a moment at ~'처럼 쓰이지 않아 문법적으로 어색함.</p></details>

**Q4.** Let's ____ — I've been staring at this screen for two hours.

- take a quick break
- take another moment
- make a good decision
- have a good talk

<details><summary>Show answer</summary><p><strong>take a quick break</strong> — 2시간 동안 화면을 본 후 잠깐 쉬자는 상황이므로 take a quick break(잠깐 휴식을 취하다)가 맞고, take another moment는 '생각할 시간이 더 필요하다'는 뜻이라 다름.</p></details>

**Q5.** I think we ____ by skipping the review process.

- made a big mistake
- made a smart choice
- made a good decision
- had a good talk

<details><summary>Show answer</summary><p><strong>made a big mistake</strong> — 검토를 건너뛴 것이 잘못된 선택임을 암시하므로 made a big mistake(큰 실수를 하다)가 맞고, made a smart choice·made a good decision은 긍정적 의미라 반대됨.</p></details>

**Q6.** Volunteering just one hour a week can really ____.

- make a big difference
- make a quick plan
- have a good sleep
- take a look

<details><summary>Show answer</summary><p><strong>make a big difference</strong> — 주 1시간 봉사가 가져오는 긍정적 파급 효과를 강조하는 맥락이므로 make a big difference(큰 차이를 만들다)가 맞고, make a quick plan은 '계획을 짜다'라 의미가 전혀 다름.</p></details>

**Q7.** We don't have much time — let's ____ and get started right away.

- make a quick plan
- make a good decision
- have a good talk
- take another moment

<details><summary>Show answer</summary><p><strong>make a quick plan</strong> — 시간이 촉박하여 빠르게 계획을 세워야 하는 상황이므로 make a quick plan(간단히 빨리 계획을 짜다)이 맞고, have a good talk는 진지한 대화로 시간이 걸리며, take another moment는 오히려 더 기다리겠다는 뜻이라 반대됨.</p></details>

**Q8.** She chose the longer but safer route, and in the end she ____.

- made a smart choice
- made a big mistake
- took a quick break
- had a good talk

<details><summary>Show answer</summary><p><strong>made a smart choice</strong> — 더 안전한 길을 선택해 좋은 결과를 얻은 상황이므로 made a smart choice(현명한 선택을 하다)가 맞고, made a big mistake는 '큰 실수'로 정반대 의미임.</p></details>
