## Session Overview

이번 세션에서는 일상 대화에서 빠르게 반응하거나 의견을 전달할 때 원어민이 즐겨 쓰는 짧은 청크 10가지를 학습했습니다. 동의·수긍("Fair enough.", "Good call."), 모름·포기("Beats me.", "It depends."), 사과·양보("My bad.", "Suit yourself.") 등 뉘앙스별로 쓰임새가 뚜렷하게 나뉩니다. 이 표현들을 상황에 맞게 구사하면 영어 대화가 훨씬 자연스럽고 유창하게 들립니다.

## 📦 Phrasal Verbs & Chunks

### It depends. — 상황에 따라 달라

- *It depends on the weather — we might cancel the trip.*
  - 날씨에 따라 달라요 — 여행을 취소할 수도 있어요.

> **A:** Can you come to my birthday dinner on Friday?
>
> **B:** It depends — I might have to work late.

### Says who? — 누가 그래?

- *Says who? I've never heard that rule before.*
  - 누가 그래요? 그런 규칙은 들어본 적 없는데요.

> **A:** You're not allowed to park here.
>
> **B:** Says who? There's no sign anywhere.

### Why not? — 안 될 게 뭐야?

- *We could try the new Thai place — why not?*
  - 새로 생긴 태국 음식점에 가봐요 — 안 될 게 뭐야?

> **A:** Should we just order pizza tonight?
>
> **B:** Why not? I'm too tired to cook anyway.

### Let's see. — 어디 보자

- *Let's see — I think the meeting starts at three.*
  - 어디 보자 — 회의가 세 시에 시작하는 것 같아요.

> **A:** Do you remember where you put my keys?
>
> **B:** Let's see — I think they're on the table.

### Fair enough. — 그럴 만하네

- *You need more time to decide? Fair enough, take the weekend.*
  - 결정할 시간이 더 필요하다고요? 그럴 만하네, 주말까지 생각해요.

> **A:** I'd rather not work on weekends if possible.
>
> **B:** Fair enough — we'll keep deadlines to weekdays.

### Beats me. — 나도 몰라

- *Beats me why the Wi-Fi keeps cutting out.*
  - 와이파이가 왜 자꾸 끊기는지 나도 몰라요.

> **A:** Why is the office so cold today?
>
> **B:** Beats me — nobody told me the AC was broken.

### Good call. — 잘 판단했어

- *Leaving early was a good call — we avoided all the traffic.*
  - 일찍 출발하길 잘 판단했어요 — 교통 체증을 다 피했어요.

> **A:** I booked the restaurant for seven instead of eight.
>
> **B:** Good call — it's always packed after eight.

### My bad. — 내 잘못이야

- *My bad — I forgot to send you the report yesterday.*
  - 내 잘못이야 — 어제 보고서 보내는 걸 잊었어요.

> **A:** You scheduled the meeting for the wrong day again.
>
> **B:** My bad — I'll reschedule it right now.

### No biggie. — 별거 아니야

- *You missed my call? No biggie — just text me later.*
  - 전화를 못 받았다고요? 별거 아니야 — 나중에 문자 보내요.

> **A:** Sorry, I accidentally deleted your message.
>
> **B:** No biggie — just send it again when you can.

### Suit yourself. — 네 맘대로 해

- *If you don't want to join us for lunch, suit yourself.*
  - 우리랑 점심 먹기 싫으면, 네 맘대로 해.

> **A:** I think I'll skip the team dinner tonight.
>
> **B:** Suit yourself — we'll bring you back some leftovers.
