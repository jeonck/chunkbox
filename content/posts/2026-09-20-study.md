이번 세션에서는 실제 도로 상황에서 자주 쓰이는 영어 청크와 구동사 9개를 학습했습니다. 끼어들기(cut in), 급정거(slam the brakes), 양보(yield)처럼 운전 중 마주치는 구체적인 행동을 영어로 표현하는 방법을 익혔습니다. 이 표현들은 영어권 국가에서 운전하거나 교통 관련 대화를 나눌 때 바로 활용할 수 있습니다.

## 📦 Phrasal Verbs & Chunks

### cut in — 끼어들다

- *That black SUV cut in right in front of me.*
  - 저 검은 SUV가 내 바로 앞으로 끼어들었어요.

> **A:** Why is everyone honking back there?
>
> **B:** Some truck just cut in without signaling.

### backed up — 막히다

- *The highway is totally backed up because of the accident.*
  - 사고 때문에 고속도로가 완전히 막혀 있어요.

> **A:** Should we take the freeway to the stadium?
>
> **B:** I wouldn't — it's backed up for miles.

### speed — 과속하다

- *He was caught speeding through a school zone.*
  - 그는 학교 구역에서 과속하다 걸렸어요.

> **A:** Why did you get a ticket?
>
> **B:** I was speeding and didn't see the sign.

### signal — 깜빡이 켜다

- *Always signal before you change lanes on the highway.*
  - 고속도로에서 차선을 바꾸기 전에 항상 깜빡이를 켜세요.

> **A:** Did you see that driver cut across three lanes?
>
> **B:** Yeah, and he didn't even signal once.

### slam the brakes — 급정거하다

- *She had to slam the brakes when a child ran out.*
  - 아이가 뛰어나오자 그녀는 급정거해야 했어요.

> **A:** Are you okay? That looked really scary.
>
> **B:** A dog ran out, so I just slammed the brakes.

### yield — 양보하다

- *You must yield to pedestrians at every crosswalk.*
  - 모든 횡단보도에서 보행자에게 양보해야 해요.

> **A:** Who has the right of way at this intersection?
>
> **B:** You have to yield — they got here first.

### back up — 후진하다

- *Can you back up a little? I need more room to park.*
  - 조금 후진해줄 수 있어요? 주차할 공간이 더 필요해서요.

> **A:** I can't get out of this tight spot.
>
> **B:** Back up slowly and turn the wheel right.

### park — 주차하다

- *It took me twenty minutes to park downtown on Friday.*
  - 금요일 시내에 주차하는 데 20분이 걸렸어요.

> **A:** There are no spots anywhere near the restaurant.
>
> **B:** Just park in the garage around the corner.

### reckless — 난폭운전하는

- *That reckless driver was weaving between lanes at full speed.*
  - 저 난폭운전자는 최고 속도로 차선을 마구 넘나들었어요.

> **A:** Did you see that car weaving all over the road?
>
> **B:** Totally reckless — he could have caused a serious crash.
