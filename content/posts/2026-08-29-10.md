## Session Overview

이번 세션에서는 'No biggie', 'Way to go!', 'For real?' 등 원어민이 일상 대화에서 즉각적으로 쓰는 짧고 강력한 반응 표현 10가지를 익혔습니다. 칭찬·위로·거절·동의·놀라움 등 다양한 상황에 맞는 청크들로, 상황을 빠르게 파악하고 자연스럽게 반응하는 회화 능력을 키울 수 있습니다. 퀴즈를 통해 각 표현이 어떤 맥락에서 쓰이는지 확인해 보세요.

## 📦 Phrasal Verbs & Chunks

### No biggie. — 별거 아니야

- *You spilled some water? No biggie, I'll clean it up.*
  - 물 좀 쏟았어? 별거 아니야, 내가 닦을게.

> **A:** Sorry, I was ten minutes late to the meeting.
>
> **B:** No biggie, we hadn't started yet.

### Way to go! — 잘했어!

- *You passed the driving test on your first try — way to go!*
  - 첫 시도에 운전면허 시험을 통과했네 — 잘했어!

> **A:** I finally finished the marathon!
>
> **B:** Way to go! I knew you could do it.

### Come on. — 에이, 왜 그래

- *Come on, you don't really believe that rumor, do you?*
  - 에이, 설마 그 소문을 진짜로 믿는 건 아니지?

> **A:** I can't go out tonight, I'm too tired.
>
> **B:** Come on, it's only for an hour!

### For real? — 진짜로?

- *They're giving out free concert tickets? For real?*
  - 콘서트 티켓을 무료로 준다고? 진짜로?

> **A:** Jake just quit his job to travel the world.
>
> **B:** For real? That's so brave!

### Makes sense. — 말 되네

- *She's been studying all night, so it makes sense she's tired.*
  - 밤새 공부했으니까 피곤한 게 말 되네.

> **A:** I think we should leave early to avoid traffic.
>
> **B:** Yeah, makes sense. Let's go at seven.

### My pleasure. — 별말씀을요

- *Thank you for helping me move. — My pleasure, anytime!*
  - 이사 도와줘서 고마워. — 별말씀을요, 언제든지!

> **A:** Thanks so much for covering my shift yesterday.
>
> **B:** My pleasure. Just let me know if you need anything.

### Not a chance. — 절대 안 되지

- *You think he'll lend you money again? Not a chance.*
  - 그가 다시 돈을 빌려줄 것 같아? 절대 안 되지.

> **A:** Do you think the boss will approve a day off tomorrow?
>
> **B:** Not a chance — it's our busiest day of the year.

### Hang in there. — 조금만 버텨

- *The semester is almost over, so hang in there a little longer.*
  - 학기가 거의 끝나가니까 조금만 더 버텨.

> **A:** Work has been so stressful lately, I'm exhausted.
>
> **B:** Hang in there — the holidays are just two weeks away.

### You got this. — 넌 할 수 있어

- *Your presentation is in five minutes — you got this!*
  - 발표가 5분 후야 — 넌 할 수 있어!

> **A:** I'm really nervous about the job interview today.
>
> **B:** You got this. You've prepared really well.

### Sounds about right. — 뭐 그럴듯하네

- *The repair will cost around two hundred dollars. — Sounds about right.*
  - 수리비가 200달러 정도 될 거야. — 뭐 그럴듯하네.

> **A:** I think the drive will take about three hours.
>
> **B:** Sounds about right, maybe a bit more with traffic.
