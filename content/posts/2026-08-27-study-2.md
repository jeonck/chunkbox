## Session Overview

이번 세션에서는 상대방의 의향을 묻는 'Do you want to ~?', 내가 도움을 줄까 제안하는 'Do you want me to ~?', 부드럽게 아이디어를 꺼내는 'I was thinking maybe we could ~' 등 실생활 회화에서 자주 등장하는 제안·의향 표현 5가지를 학습했습니다. 또한 과거의 반복적 습관을 나타내는 'I used to ~'와 현재 혼자 고려 중임을 표현하는 'I'm thinking of ~'도 익혔습니다. 마지막으로 실수나 오해를 사과할 때 쓰는 'I didn't mean to ~'와 줄여 쓰는 'didn't mean to'의 미묘한 차이도 다뤘습니다.

## 📦 Phrasal Verbs & Chunks

### Do you want to ~? — 상대방의 의향 묻기

- *Do you want to grab some coffee after class?*
  - 수업 끝나고 커피 마실래?

> **A:** Do you want to join us for dinner tonight?
>
> **B:** Sure, I'd love to! What time?

### Do you want me to ~? — 내가 ~해주길 바라?

- *Do you want me to pick you up from the airport?*
  - 내가 공항에 마중 나가줄까?

> **A:** Do you want me to explain the instructions again?
>
> **B:** Yes, please — I was a bit confused.

### I used to ~ — 예전에 ~하곤 했다

- *I used to walk to school every morning.*
  - 나는 예전에 매일 아침 학교까지 걸어 다니곤 했어.

> **A:** You seem really comfortable speaking English now.
>
> **B:** Thanks! I used to be too shy to say anything.

### I didn't mean to ~ — ~하려던 건 아니었어

- *I didn't mean to interrupt your conversation.*
  - 네 대화를 끊으려던 건 아니었어.

> **A:** I didn't mean to hurt your feelings — I'm really sorry.
>
> **B:** It's okay, I know you didn't do it on purpose.

### didn't mean to — ~하려던 게 아니었어, 고의가 아니었어

- *I didn't mean to interrupt — please keep talking.*
  - 끼어들려던 게 아니었어 — 계속 말해.

> **A:** Why did you share that story without asking me first?
>
> **B:** I didn't mean to embarrass you — I'm really sorry.

### I was thinking maybe we could ~ — 부드럽게 제안하기

- *I was thinking maybe we could grab dinner sometime.*
  - 혹시 우리 언제 한번 같이 저녁 먹을 수 있지 않을까 해서.

> **A:** Do you have any plans this weekend?
>
> **B:** I was thinking maybe we could check out that new café.

### I'm thinking of ~ — ~할까 생각 중이야

- *I'm thinking of moving to a smaller apartment next year.*
  - 내년에 더 작은 아파트로 이사할까 생각 중이야.

> **A:** Have you decided on your next career move yet?
>
> **B:** Not yet — I'm thinking of going back to school.

## ✅ Check Yourself

**Q1.** A: _____ try the new ramen place downtown for lunch?

- Do you want to
- Do you want me to
- I'm thinking of
- I used to

<details><summary>Show answer</summary><p><strong>Do you want to</strong> — 'Do you want to ~?'는 상대방이 ~하고 싶은지 묻는 표현이고, 'Do you want me to ~?'는 내가 해줄까 제안하는 것이라 주체가 달라요.</p></details>

**Q2.** You seem overwhelmed. _____ take some of those tasks off your plate?

- Do you want me to
- I was thinking maybe we could
- I didn't mean to
- I used to

<details><summary>Show answer</summary><p><strong>Do you want me to</strong> — 'Do you want me to ~?'는 내(화자)가 도움을 제공할지 묻는 표현이며, 나머지는 이 맥락의 의미나 문법에 맞지 않아요.</p></details>

**Q3.** _____ jog every morning, but I hurt my knee last year and had to stop.

- I used to
- I'm thinking of
- I was thinking maybe we could
- didn't mean to

<details><summary>Show answer</summary><p><strong>I used to</strong> — 'I used to ~'는 과거의 반복적인 습관을 나타내는 유일한 표현이고, 나머지는 제안이나 의도를 뜻해요.</p></details>

**Q4.** _____ make you cry. I was just trying to be honest.

- I didn't mean to

- I'm thinking of

<details><summary>Show answer</summary><p><strong>I didn't mean to</strong> — 'I didn't mean to ~'는 어떤 행동이 고의가 아니었음을 설명하는 완전한 문장 표현이며, 나머지는 이 상황에 전혀 어울리지 않아요.</p></details>

**Q5.** He bumped into her in the hallway and immediately said, 'Sorry! I _____!'

- I'm thinking of
